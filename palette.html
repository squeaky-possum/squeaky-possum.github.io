03Method, and one admission

Each token is resolved by setting it as the color of a hidden probe element and reading back the computed value, so anything the stylesheet does with color-mix() is resolved by the browser rather than guessed by me.

Relative luminance and the ratio follow the WCAG 2.1 definitions exactly: sRGB channels are linearised, weighted 0.2126 / 0.7152 / 0.0722, and the ratio is (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05). No rounding until the last step.

The admission: the first time I ran this, control borders came out at 1.8:1 — under the 3:1 minimum, because I had picked a pleasant-looking grey instead of a testable one. I darkened --border-strong in both palettes until it passed. That is the whole story of this page, and it is why the site has an audit at all.
