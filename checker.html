06What the numbers mean

Normal text is anything under 24px, or under 18.66px when bold. That is most of the text on most sites, which is why 4.5:1 is the number to design against.

Large text gets 3:1 because the letters are big enough to be resolved without as much contrast. It is not permission to let body copy go grey.

Borders and focus rings are non-text: WCAG asks for 3:1 so a control can be seen at all. Decorative hairlines are exempt, which is a distinction designers frequently blur in the wrong direction.

The suggested colour moves lightness in OKLab, not HSL, so the hue stays put while the pair becomes legible. It is the smallest change I could find on an 8-bit grid, not the mathematically perfect minimum — rounding to a screen colour costs a step now and then.
